# Image Identity — El Desquite

Literary site for **El Desquite** under the **Garitma** umbrella. The primary surface is a **typographic statement**, not a marketing collage.

## Style

- Cool paper and ink; quiet grain instead of stock photography
- Sharp crimson accent as the only brand mark of heat
- Prefer empty space and letterform over decorative objects

## Palette

- Canvas: Aura `gray-1` / `gray-2` (cool zinc paper)
- Ink: Aura `gray-12`
- Accent: Aura `accent-9` (`#b42318`) for rules, links, and emphasis
- Avoid purple SaaS gradients, cream+terracotta stacks, and neon glow

## Composition / motifs

- Full-bleed atmospheric field behind a single brand statement
- Thin accent rule under the title
- Chapter list as a quiet archive, not a card grid

## Exclusions

- No floating badges, promo chips, or sticker overlays
- No inset hero media cards
- No Inter / system UI as the display voice
